Studio1 ran a **full-stack growth campaign** for **ScrapeGraphAI** across content, community, and product: built to increase awareness, drive real developer usage, and improve the onboarding experience for its AI scraping and data extraction platform.

The campaign combined **multi-channel distribution**, **hands-on AI agent projects**, **event activation**, and **product feedback loops**, positioning ScrapeGraphAI as a practical, production-ready tool for building AI-powered scraping and research workflows, and reaching **265K+ developers** across social and community platforms.

## About ScrapeGraphAI

[**ScrapeGraphAI**](https://dub.sh/scrapegraph) is a developer-first AI scraping and data extraction platform that lets teams build intelligent agents for web research, structured data collection, and automation. It provides:

* AI-powered web scraping
* Structured data extraction
* Browser automation
* API-based access to scraped data

Because the product sits at the intersection of AI agents and data infrastructure, developers need to see it solving real problems in production, not read a feature list, before they trust it inside their own stack.

## The challenge: proving real-world use over feature marketing

ScrapeGraphAI needed a campaign that could:

* Increase **awareness and reach** across developer communities beyond its existing audience
* Show **real, practical use-cases** in place of abstract feature marketing
* Drive **hands-on adoption** of its SDK and APIs
* Improve **onboarding, docs, and product experience** for new users
* Strengthen positioning with its ICP: **AI engineers, founders, and agent builders**

The brief wasn't more content. It was a **campaign that connected product, community, and real developer workflows** into one system.

## Our role

Studio1 worked as ScrapeGraphAI's embedded growth partner, owning:

* **Multi-channel distribution**: placing content where developers already discover tools, across Reddit, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, and newsletter
* **Demo engineering**: four hands-on AI agent projects built around real production use-cases
* **Visual storytelling**: illustrations and explainers that made agent architecture legible at a glance
* **Event presence**: in-person developer activation on the ground in Hyderabad
* **Product feedback loops**: a structured product and DevEx audit feeding straight back into onboarding, docs, and UX

Every channel was built to produce a real workflow example, not just an impression.

## What we delivered

### 1. Multi-channel distribution

We launched ScrapeGraphAI content across the surfaces developers already use to discover tools and technical content:

* YouTube + Newsletter: **4.8K+**
* X (Twitter) + LinkedIn: **90K+**
* Reddit: **~170K+**

Combined, these channels put ScrapeGraphAI in front of **265K+ developers**: showing up where technical audiences already spend time, instead of asking them to come find it.

### 2. Four real AI agent projects

Instead of generic demos, we built **four concrete AI agent projects** around ScrapeGraphAI's real-world use-cases:

* Deep research agents
* Price and data monitoring
* Structured extraction pipelines
* AI-powered discovery workflows

Each project was scoped to answer one question clearly: **"How would a real developer actually use this in production?"**

### 3. Social launches on X and Reddit

Every project launched on **X** and was shared across **Reddit** with high-signal posts covering the problem being solved, the agent architecture, why ScrapeGraphAI fit the use-case, and practical implementation insights.

### 6. Product, onboarding, and docs feedback

Beyond content, Studio1 worked as a **product growth partner**, helping ScrapeGraphAI improve key parts of its product, onboarding, and developer experience based on real usage. We supported improvements across:

* **Onboarding & activation**: clearer first-time flows, better credit visibility, and a smoother setup experience
* **Documentation & learning**: more use-case-driven docs, practical SDK examples, and clearer workflows
* **Product & playground UX**: UI refinements, better error handling, and a more intuitive testing experience
* **Growth mechanics**: a stronger free tier, credit-based activation loops, and clearer product messaging

All of it was delivered as a **structured product and DevEx audit**, aligned to how real developers discover, test, and adopt ScrapeGraphAI.

## The results

* **265K+ total reach** across community platforms: ~170K+ on Reddit, 90K+ on X and LinkedIn, and 4.8K+ on YouTube and newsletter
* **High-signal technical discussions** driven by real projects instead of promotional posts
* **Strong inbound interest** from AI engineers and founders
* **Clearer product positioning** built around real-world use-cases
* **Actionable improvements** to onboarding, docs, and product UX from the DevEx audit

Instead of vanity metrics, the focus stayed on **real developer adoption, product clarity, and long-term growth foundations**.

## Why it worked

The campaign worked because distribution, demos, events, and feedback were treated as **one connected system**: multi-channel launches created reach, four real agent projects gave that reach something concrete to evaluate, the Hyderabad event turned attention into direct relationships, and the product audit converted all of it into decisions the ScrapeGraphAI team could act on. That's what connects product, community, and real developer workflows, not a content calendar running in parallel with the roadmap, but one motion feeding the other.
