Studio1 partnered with **ScrapeGraph AI** to run a full-stack growth campaign across content, community, and product—focused on increasing awareness, driving real developer usage, and improving the onboarding experience.

The campaign combined **multi-channel distribution**, **hands-on AI agent projects**, **event activation**, and **product feedback loops** to position ScrapeGraph AI as a practical, production-ready tool for building AI-powered scraping and research workflows.

Key outcomes included:

* ~265K+ total reach across social and community platforms

* Real-world adoption driven by four concrete AI agent examples
* Product and documentation improvements aligned with ICP needs

## What is ScrapeGraph AI?

[**ScrapeGraph AI**](https://dub.sh/scrapegraph) is a developer-first AI scraping and data extraction platform that enables teams to build intelligent agents for web research, structured data collection, and automation.

It provides:

* AI-powered web scraping
* Structured data extraction
* Browser automation
* API-based access to scraped data

## The challenge

ScrapeGraph AI wanted to:

* Increase **awareness and reach** across developer communities.
* Show **real, practical use-cases** instead of abstract feature marketing.
* Drive **hands-on adoption** of their SDK and APIs.
* Improve **onboarding, docs, and product experience** for new users.
* Strengthen positioning with their ideal ICP (AI engineers, founders, agent builders).

They needed not just content, but a **campaign that connects product, community, and real developer workflows**.

## What Studio1 delivered

Studio1 executed a full growth campaign across **content, social, community, events, and product feedback**.

### 1. Multi-channel distribution campaign

We launched ScrapeGraph AI content across major developer surfaces:

**Reach summary:**

* YouTube + Newsletter: **4.8K+**
* Twitter (X) + LinkedIn: **90K+**
* Reddit: **~170K+**

This ensured ScrapeGraph AI showed up where developers already consume technical content and discover new tools.

### 2. Four real AI agent examples (use-case driven)

Instead of generic demos, Studio1 built **four concrete AI agent projects** focused on ScrapeGraph AI's real-world use-cases.

These examples showcased how ScrapeGraph can be used for:

* Deep research agents
* Price and data monitoring
* Structured extraction pipelines
* AI-powered discovery workflows

Each project was designed to answer one question clearly: **"How would a real developer actually use this in production?"**

### 3. Social launches on X and Reddit

All projects were launched on **X (Twitter)** and shared across Reddit with high-signal posts explaining:

* The problem being solved
* The agent architecture
* Why ScrapeGraph AI fits the use-case

These posts generated strong inbound engagement and organic discussions.

### 6. Product, onboarding, and docs feedback

Beyond content, Studio1 worked as a product growth partner, helping ScrapeGraph AI improve key parts of their product, onboarding, and developer experience based on real usage.

We supported improvements across:

* Onboarding & activation: clearer first-time flows, better credit visibility, and smoother setup experience
* Documentation & learning: more use-case-driven docs, practical SDK examples, and clearer workflows
* Product & playground UX: UI refinements, better error handling, and more intuitive testing experience
* Growth mechanics: stronger free tier, credit-based activation loops, and clearer product messaging

All insights were shared as a structured product and DevEx audit, aligned to how real developers discover, test, and adopt ScrapeGraph AI.
