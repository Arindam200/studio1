Studio1 is looking for a Growth Intern who can help us get more clients, support Raah and AssetSnip growth, and keep internal execution moving. This is a founder's-office style role across sales, marketing, research, operations, and coordination.

## What you will do

You will help the founders with both growth and operations, from finding leads to keeping team tasks moving.

- Research potential clients across devtools, AI, SaaS, and open-source companies.
- Support outreach for Studio1 services, Raah, and AssetSnip through lists, messages, and follow-ups.
- Help with CRM updates, lead tracking, task coordination, and founder follow-ups.

## What we are looking for

You do not need to be a sales expert. You should be organized, curious, and comfortable talking to people.

- You are interested in startups, SaaS, AI, devtools, sales, marketing, or operations.
- You can research companies, decision-makers, communities, and relevant context.
- You are comfortable with docs, spreadsheets, task tools, async updates, and follow-ups.

## Why join

You will work close to the founders and see how a small tech company actually grows. You will build practical experience across sales, marketing, operations, and product growth.

## Internship details

This is a 3-month remote internship for India-based candidates. Best fit: someone organized and curious who wants broad exposure to growth, sales, and startup operations.

## How to apply

Use the Apply button and keep the subject line as: `Application: Growth Intern`.

Include your resume and proof of work: outreach samples, projects, research docs, sales experience, community work, or anything that shows you can execute. We do not expect you to know everything, but we do want proof that you can learn, organize, and move fast.
