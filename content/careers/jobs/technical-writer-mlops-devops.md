Studio1 partners with infrastructure and platform teams to ship documentation and technical content that developers actually use. We're hiring a Technical Writer with MLOps/DevOps depth to produce guides, runbooks, and product content for engineering audiences.

## What you'll do

- Create documentation for deployment pipelines, observability, CI/CD, and ML platform workflows.
- Write practical guides that help teams adopt new tooling—from install steps to production patterns.
- Collaborate with client engineering and DevRel teams to capture accurate technical details and edge cases.
- Develop internal cookbooks, integration guides, and troubleshooting docs for platform products.
- Turn dense infrastructure concepts into scannable docs with strong information architecture.

## What we're looking for

- Proven experience writing for DevOps, platform, or MLOps audiences.
- Understanding of containers, Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, and ML lifecycle tooling.
- Clear, concise writing style with examples, diagrams, and step-by-step structure.
- Comfort interviewing engineers and translating their knowledge into publishable content.
- Reliable async communication and ability to manage multiple client projects.

## Nice to have

- Hands-on experience with Terraform, Helm, GitHub Actions, or similar automation tools.
- Familiarity with model serving, feature stores, experiment tracking, or ML monitoring.
- Prior technical writing for SaaS, open-source, or developer infrastructure companies.
- Part-time availability is welcome; we can scope engagement accordingly.

## How we work

- Remote-first with flexible scheduling; most collaboration happens over docs, Slack, and brief calls.
- You'll work across client accounts—platform startups, devtools, and ML infrastructure teams.
- Studio1 provides editorial direction, style guidance, and client context so you can focus on craft.

## Benefits

- Remote work with meaningful ownership over deliverables.
- Exposure to a wide range of infrastructure and MLOps products.
- Supportive team that values clarity, accuracy, and developer empathy.
- Path to expanded scope for writers who want to lead docs programs or content strategy.
