Studio1 helps AI and developer-tool companies turn complex product stories into content developers trust. We're looking for a Technical Writer focused on AI/ML to create tutorials, launch assets, and educational content that drives adoption.

## What you'll do

- Write long-form tutorials, quickstarts, and integration guides for AI/ML products aimed at developers and ML engineers.
- Partner with founders, DevRel, and engineering to translate model capabilities, APIs, and workflows into clear narratives.
- Produce launch content for product releases, feature announcements, and developer campaigns.
- Edit and improve existing docs for clarity, structure, and consistency across SDK references and conceptual guides.
- Research audience pain points and shape content that meets developers where they are—from first API call to production deployment.

## What we're looking for

- Strong writing portfolio with technical samples (AI/ML, LLMs, data tooling, or adjacent developer products).
- Comfort explaining models, embeddings, fine-tuning, inference, and ML infrastructure without oversimplifying.
- Ability to work independently in a remote, async environment with minimal hand-holding.
- Experience writing for developers—code samples, API docs, or hands-on tutorials are a plus.
- Curiosity about the AI ecosystem and willingness to learn new tools quickly.

## Nice to have

- Background in software engineering, data science, or ML engineering.
- Familiarity with Python, Jupyter notebooks, Hugging Face, LangChain, or similar stacks.
- Experience publishing on Dev.to, Hashnode, company blogs, or documentation platforms.
- Prior work with open-source or developer-first startups.

## How we work

- Remote-first team across time zones; we optimize for written communication and clear briefs.
- Small team, high trust—you'll own drafts end-to-end with editorial support from Studio1 leads.
- Projects span multiple clients; you'll rotate across AI infra, agent frameworks, and devtool launches.

## Benefits

- Fully remote with flexible hours.
- Work on products shaping how developers build with AI.
- Direct collaboration with technical founders and experienced DevRel practitioners.
- Room to grow into lead writer or content strategy roles as the team scales.
