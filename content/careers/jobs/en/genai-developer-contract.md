Studio1 will soon open a contract role for GenAI Developers who can build fast, creative AI prototypes and agentic apps. This is for builders who like trying new frameworks, shipping demos, and turning ideas into working projects people can actually use or inspect.

This is not a senior engineering role or a generic coding internship. It is best for CSE students, college builders, and entry-level developers who are already shipping cool demos, open-source repos, or prototype apps and want to build faster around AI.

## What you will do

You will help Studio1 experiment with AI product ideas, demos, and developer-facing prototypes across our internal products and client work.

- Build agentic apps, GenAI demos, AI workflows, and prototype tools.
- Try new frameworks, SDKs, model APIs, agent libraries, and developer tools fast.
- Ship clean demo repos with useful READMEs, setup steps, and examples.
- Turn rough product ideas into working prototypes that can be tested quickly.
- Support technical content with code demos, sample apps, and experiment repos when needed.

## What we are looking for

You should be comfortable building projects, learning new stacks, and sharing proof of what you have shipped.

- You have live projects, open-source repos, demos, or prototypes that show your building ability.
- Impressive projects can include an open-source alternative to an AI tool, a useful agent implementation with interesting tools, or a creative workflow that solves a real developer or product problem.
- You can learn new AI frameworks, SDKs, and APIs quickly without waiting for step-by-step instructions.
- You can build practical demos around agents, LLMs, RAG, automation, devtools, SaaS, or AI workflows without making generic wrapper-style agents.
- You care about clean enough code, clear READMEs, and projects that others can run or understand.
- Any social proof, community response, GitHub stars, product launch, or virality around your projects is a strong plus.

## Why work with us

You will get room to build visible AI experiments, learn fast, and work around developer products, AI tooling, content, and launch ideas. This is a good fit for builders who want a stronger portfolio and like shipping more than talking.

## Contract details

This is a remote contract role for 2 people based in India. It is best suited for CSE students, college builders, and entry-level developers with strong project proof. Applications will open soon.

## How to apply

Applications for this contract role will open soon.

When applications open, send your resume or profile, live project links, open-source repos, demo links, and any proof of traction or social response your projects created.

Once applications open, response time will usually be 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for project proof because this role is about shipping fast prototypes, not only knowing tools in theory.**
