Studio1 needs a Visual & Video Content Intern who can turn rough ideas into clean visuals, scripts, reels, and short videos. You will work with the social content intern to grow Studio1 socials and support product content for Raah and AssetSnip.

## What you will do

You will own the visual and production side of social growth, from scripts and edits to scheduled assets.

- Create scripts, reels, carousels, visual explainers, thumbnails, short videos, and campaign graphics.
- Use AI creative tools such as HeyGen, Higgsfield, or similar video tools as part of the workflow.
- Package, schedule, and repurpose blogs, docs, product updates, and launch ideas into polished social assets.

## What we are looking for

You do not need agency experience. You should have taste, speed, and proof that you like making visual content.

- You have created reels, edits, carousels, thumbnails, motion posts, or design samples.
- You are comfortable using AI video or design tools, especially HeyGen-style tools.
- You can take a rough content idea and make it look clear, sharp, and shareable.

## Why join

You will work as one half of Studio1's content team with the social content intern. You will build a visible portfolio around AI, devtools, SaaS, launches, and social growth.

## Internship details

This is a 3-month remote internship for India-based candidates. Best fit: someone who enjoys making visuals or videos and wants to learn tech marketing by shipping.

## How to apply

Use the Apply button and keep the subject line as: `Application: Visual Video Intern`.

Include your resume and proof of work: portfolio links, scripts, reels, thumbnails, carousels, videos, design samples, or experiments with AI video tools. We will prioritize candidates who show taste, consistency, and willingness to learn.
