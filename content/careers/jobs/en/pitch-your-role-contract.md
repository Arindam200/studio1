Studio1 is open to hearing from people who can bring a specific, relevant skill that does not fit one of the listed roles. This is for candidates who can clearly explain what they can help us with and show proof that they can execute.

Because this is open-ended, relevance matters a lot. We are a small team, so a strong application should make it easy for us to understand the role you are pitching, why Studio1 needs it, and what you can deliver.

## What you can pitch

You can pitch a contract role if you can help Studio1, Raah, AssetSnip, or our client work in a clear and practical way.

- A specific function, project, or workflow you can own.
- A clear problem you noticed and how you would solve it.
- A service, growth, content, community, product, design, engineering, or operations contribution that is relevant to Studio1.
- A short plan for what you would do in the first 2-4 weeks.

## What we are looking for

This is not for generic applications. You should come with a clear point of view and proof.

- You understand what Studio1 does and why your pitch is relevant.
- You can share proof of past work, projects, results, writing, designs, code, communities, campaigns, or operations work.
- You can explain what you would own, how you would work, and what success could look like.
- A tech or software background helps a lot, but curiosity and willingness to learn matter too.

## Contract details

This is a remote contract opportunity. Scope, timeline, and workload will depend on the role you pitch and whether it fits current Studio1 priorities.

## How to apply

Use the Apply button and keep the subject line as: `Application: Pitch Your Role`.

Include your resume or profile, proof of past work, and one clean single-page document explaining the role you are pitching, what you can help with, why it matters for Studio1, and what you would do first.

If this application is used to apply for an already listed role instead of pitching a new relevant role, there will be no response. Please apply to the correct role when one already fits.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for a focused pitch because open-ended applications only work when the relevance, proof, and value are clear.**
