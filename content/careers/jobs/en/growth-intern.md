Studio1 is looking for a Growth & Marketing Intern who can help us get more clients, support Raah and AssetSnip growth, research software companies, and keep internal execution moving. This is a founder's-office style role across sales outreach, marketing, research, operations, and coordination.

## What you will do

You will help the founders with growth, marketing, and operations, from finding leads to packaging campaigns and keeping team tasks moving.

- Research potential clients across devtools, AI, SaaS, and open-source companies.
- Support outreach for Studio1 services, Raah, and AssetSnip through lists, messages, and follow-ups.
- Research competitors, creators, communities, and campaign ideas across AI, SaaS, devtools, and startup audiences.
- Help with CRM updates, lead tracking, product marketing notes, task coordination, and founder follow-ups using tools like Notion, Linear, spreadsheets, and simple CRM workflows.

## What we are looking for

You do not need to be a sales expert. You should be organized, curious, and comfortable talking to people.

- You are interested in startups, SaaS, AI, devtools, sales, marketing, or operations.
- A tech or software background helps a lot, but curiosity and willingness to learn matter too.
- You can research companies, decision-makers, communities, and relevant context.
- You have been active in student tech communities, clubs, hackathons, events, or startup/operator circles.
- You have good communication skills because this role includes client outreach, follow-ups, and coordination.
- You are comfortable with docs, spreadsheets, Notion, Linear or similar task tools, async updates, and follow-ups.
- You have some interest or experience in SEO writing, marketing content, landing page notes, campaign copy, or product messaging.

## Why join

You will work close to the founders and see how a small tech company actually grows. You will build practical experience across sales, client outreach, marketing, operations, and product growth.

## Internship details

This is a 3-month remote internship for India-based candidates, with extension possible based on performance and work quality. Best fit: students or early-career operators who have worked around tech communities, organized hackathons or events, helped clubs grow, or supported startup/community initiatives.

## How to apply

Use the Apply button and keep the subject line as: `Application: Growth Marketing Intern`.

Include your resume, past work links if you have them, and one clean short doc with a 1-week plan for how you would help Studio1 get more clients or grow Raah and AssetSnip. This can include outreach ideas, target company research, campaign ideas, partnerships, communities, or simple growth experiments.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for this because even a few minutes spent understanding Studio1 before applying shows you are serious, willing to make an effort, and ready to learn with us.**
