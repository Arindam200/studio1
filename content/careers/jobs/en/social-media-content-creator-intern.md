Studio1 works with devtool, AI, and SaaS teams on technical content, DevRel, product launches, docs, and developer growth. We also build products like Raah and AssetSnip, so this role will help manage and grow Studio1 and product socials through written content and community activity.

## What you will do

You will own the written and community side of social growth while working closely with the visual/video intern.

- Create posts, hooks, memes, threads, captions, and community prompts for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Reddit, and developer communities.
- Research new AI products, devtool launches, social trends, and developer memes to find content angles.
- Manage the weekly social calendar, schedule posts, and help grow Studio1 community channels.

## What we are looking for

You do not need to know everything. You should be curious, online, and able to show that you have tried writing or managing content before.

- You follow tech, AI, startups, creators, or developer culture closely.
- You have proof of written content from personal pages, campus pages, communities, newsletters, or past projects.
- You can spot trends early, write simple hooks, and turn rough ideas into posts quickly.

## Why join

You will work as one half of Studio1's content team with the visual/video intern. If you are good at writing or SEO, you may also help with Studio1 blog ideas and drafts.

## Internship details

This is a 3-month remote internship for India-based candidates. Best fit: someone who spends real time on social platforms and wants to learn tech content by doing.

## How to apply

Use the Apply button and keep the subject line as: `Application: Social Content Intern`.

Include your resume and proof of work: links to social profiles, posts, threads, communities, newsletters, blogs, or any content you have created. We care more about proof, taste, and consistency than formal experience.
